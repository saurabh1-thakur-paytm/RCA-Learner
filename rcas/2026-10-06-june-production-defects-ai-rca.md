## Summary
This is a defect-level RCA log of 41 production and pre-production defects (METAC-24767 to METAC-26843) across the LCPR / Puerto Rico Green checkout, PLP, KYC, analytics/dataLayer, integrations (Salesforce, Hybris, Alviere, Nokia/ESB, Emarsys, USPS/Melissa) and storage handling. For each defect, the sheet records the module, environment, severity, root cause, detailed RCA, where the defect should have been caught, why it escaped, the preventive action, and the owner. It is a multi-defect analysis, not a single-incident RCA.

Recurring themes stated across rows: missing negative/edge-case test coverage (error paths, restricted storage, prepaid users, credit-score letters), contract/mapping gaps between BFF, Hybris and Salesforce, localization keys hardcoded or missing, environment/maintenance-window outages without gating, and soft-fail error handling that silently routes users to callback (C2C).

## Impact
- Severity breakdown (as labelled in the sheet): Medium: 15, High: 13, Low: 10, High (during spike window): 2, Highest: 1
